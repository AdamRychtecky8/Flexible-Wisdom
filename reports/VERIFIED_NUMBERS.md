## 5. Weighted Linear Combination (WLC) vs Majority Voting

**Source:** `outputs/wlc-cv-results.csv` — generated by `notebooks/Main-Analysis.ipynb`.  
**CV setup:** 10-fold, sequential (non-random) folds based on contiguous stimID blocks. Human input: confidence ratings (response, 1–7 scale, normalized to [0,1]). Model input: binary decisions (0/1). WLC trained with log-odds least-squares; threshold chosen to maximize training accuracy.

> **ANOMALY — flag for review:** The WLC as implemented in Main-Analysis.ipynb produces accuracy *below* majority voting for both humans and LLMs in all conditions. This is unexpected. The most likely cause is a numerical instability when applying a log-odds transform to binary model decisions (all values collapse to ±13.8 before weighting). The reported numbers below are computed correctly by the notebook; the anomaly is in the method design, not the arithmetic. A separate WLC implementation exists in `notebooks/Mod-Hum-Comparison.ipynb` (not part of the specified pipeline) and was not run.
